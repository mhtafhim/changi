  1. 19:55

    Reach the Terminal 2 driveway

    Use a free trolley. Keep your passport, booking and entry documents out—not inside checked baggage.

  2. 20:00

    Enter through the displayed SQ 447 gate

    Show passport and ticket to airport security, then put all luggage through the entrance X-ray.

  3. 20:20

    Find Singapore Airlines and check in

    Ask for both SQ 447 and SQ 326 boarding passes. Confirm every checked-bag tag says FRA, then photograph the baggage receipt.

  4. 21:15

    Complete Bangladesh emigration

    Follow Immigration 1, 2 or 3. Bangladeshi passport holders do not need the departure immigration card, according to HSIA.

  5. 21:45

    Pass final security

    Place jacket, belt, shoes, phone, laptop, chargers, power bank, keys and coins in the trays when directed.

  6. 22:15

    Be at the boarding gate

    Recheck the screen, fill your bottle after security if permitted, use the washroom and stay close to the gate.

  7. 23:15

    Board SQ 447 when called

    Keep the FRA bag receipt and SQ 326 boarding pass with your passport. Set your phone to Singapore time after takeoff.

  8. 23:55

    Depart for Singapore

    Scheduled flight time: 4 hours 05 minutes. Arrival is 06:00 Singapore time because Singapore is two hours ahead.

HSIA recommends reaching the departure driveway and check-in area four hours before an international flight and proceeding to the gate 1–2 hours before departure.

    If you have a connecting flight from Frankfurt, do not follow this exit plan—follow Transfer signs instead.

    1. 19:40

      Land at Frankfurt Terminal 1

      After leaving the aircraft, follow Arrivals / Ankunft and Baggage Claim / Gepäckausgabe—not Transfer.

    2. 20:00

      Join non-EU border control

      Use the EES kiosk if staff direct you, then present your passport and short-stay visa to the officer. Be ready with your address, visit purpose, return plan and proof of funds.

    3. 20:45

      Collect your checked bag

      Find SQ 326 on the baggage screens and match the bag-tag number before leaving the carousel.

    4. 21:10

      Report a missing or damaged bag now

      If needed, visit Singapore Airlines’ baggage-service desk before customs. Keep the boarding pass and baggage receipt.

    5. 21:15

      Choose the correct customs exit

      Use green only when you have nothing to declare. Use red when carrying goods, cash or quantities that require declaration—or when unsure.

    6. 21:25

      Exit into the Terminal 1 arrivals hall

      You are now landside in Germany. Follow signs for regional trains, long-distance trains, taxis or your pickup point.

    Arrival times are planning estimates, not guarantees. EES queues and baggage delivery may add time; do not book a non-flexible onward train too close to landing.
